How to use each device mode safely with your skincare products
💧 Booster Mode
Uses vibration + micro-current to press water-based products deeper. Best after toner is applied to skin.
Toner Essence Calming Serum HA Serum
⛔ Avoid: retinol, AHA, BHA, peeling gel same day
🟣 MC Mode
Micro-current mode. Pairs with PDRN gel. For post-acne marks, lifting, texture. Phase 2+ only.
PDRN Booster Gel Conductive Gel
⛔ Never on active pimples · Phase 2+ · Not with retinol
🔴 Derma Shot
Pneumatic pressure for deeper penetration. Best for PIH marks when barrier is fully repaired. Phase 2+.
PDRN Booster Gel
⛔ Never on broken/inflamed skin · Max 2 min · Phase 2+
💨 Air Shot
Pressurised air on completely dry, clean skin BEFORE any product. 1–2 passes max for sensitive skin.
No product — dry skin only
⛔ Never same day as retinol, AHA, BHA, peeling gel · Mature/sensitive: use rarely
